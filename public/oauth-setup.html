Google OAuth Setup

One-time setup to authorize Search Console access for the Balboa platform.
Run this page locally at http://localhost:3000 — never deploy it.

⚠️ This page must be opened at http://localhost:3000/oauth-setup — the OAuth redirect URI is registered to that origin. Open it any other way and the callback won't work.

Step 1 — Authorize with Google

Click the button below. Google will ask you to sign in and grant Search Console read access. You'll be redirected back here automatically.

Sign in with the Google account that owns the Search Console properties.

Two kinds of mispricing, side by side. Market-rent deviation compares each floorplan's market $/SF to its tier average — it flags where Greystar's asking rent looks internally inconsistent. Loss-to-lease deviation compares the floorplan's in-place gap to the tier average — it flags where actual leases lag regardless of whether asking is right. Comparison is within tier (Small / 1BR / 2BR / 3BR), $/SF-based so unit size doesn't create false flags.

Current exposure reads off the latest rent roll — units at risk of becoming vacant, as a % of GPR. Vacant and notice-to-vacate are hard counts (a move-out date is explicit). Undecided is inferred — occupied, no notice, lease expiring within ${'60'} days, not yet renewed — a reasonable proxy, not a Yardi status field.
